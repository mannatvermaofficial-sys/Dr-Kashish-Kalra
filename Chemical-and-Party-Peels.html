Procedures  /  Chemical & Party Peels
Medifacial

Peels, at the right depth.

A peel is a controlled exfoliation. Depth is everything — and on Indian skin, too deep means pigmentation, not glow.

How it works

Illustrative image, generated with AI. Not a patient of the clinic and not a treatment result.

Overview

Depth chosen for your skin.

Chemical peels apply an acid solution to remove surface layers in a controlled way, prompting fresher skin to replace them. Superficial 'party peels' give a quick radiance boost with minimal flaking; medium-depth peels do more but require recovery.

On deeper skin tones, choosing depth conservatively matters a great deal. Over-aggressive peeling is a common cause of post-inflammatory pigmentation — which is the opposite of what people came for.

Concerns this may address
Dull skinUneven toneMild pigmentationCongested skin and blackheadsPost-acne marksRough texturePre-event brightening
Suitability

Who it may — and may not — suit.

May be considered if

  • You want brighter, more even skin
  • You have mild pigmentation or post-acne marks
  • You can follow sun-protection instructions afterwards
  • You want a treatment with a defined course

Usually assessed first if

  • You have active inflamed acne or infection
  • You cannot avoid sun exposure afterwards
  • You have recently used isotretinoin — timing is assessed
  • You have a history of pigmentation after procedures — depth is reduced accordingly

This is general information, not a diagnosis. Suitability is confirmed only after an in-person assessment.

What To Expect

Honest expectations, set early.

Superficial peels cause mild flaking for two to four days with brightness emerging after. Medium peels involve more visible peeling for roughly five to seven days.

A course of several peels spaced two to four weeks apart gives far better results than a single session. Sun protection afterwards is non-negotiable.

Individual experiences and outcomes vary. Timelines are confirmed and medically reviewed at consultation.

Cost, Honestly

Why a price follows the plan.

An honest estimate depends on your assessment and plan — not a single photo or phone call. It may depend on:

  • Peel type and depth — Superficial, medium and combination peels differ.
  • Number of sessions — A course is usual for pigmentation or marks.
  • Priming skincare — Pre-peel preparation where indicated.
  • Areas treated — Face, neck, back or hands.
