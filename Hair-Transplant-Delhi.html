Hair Transplant in Delhi

Should you actually get a hair transplant?

Take a private two-minute readiness check. It will not diagnose you or approve surgery. It will help you understand the right next conversation.

First, understand how candidacy works

No phone number. No photographs. No automatic WhatsApp message.

This is a Readiness Check — not a diagnosis, eligibility test, or graft calculator. It cannot examine your scalp or confirm that surgery is appropriate. Only an in-person clinical and donor-area assessment can determine candidacy.

What Determines Candidacy

A hairline is designed. Candidacy is diagnosed.

01

Cause of hair loss

Similar-looking loss can have different causes. The diagnosis, not the appearance, decides whether surgery is even the right conversation.

02

Pattern & stability

Where and how fast hair is changing matters. Actively progressing loss is planned differently from stable, established patterns.

03

Donor density & miniaturisation

The back and sides are a finite supply. Their density and quality set the realistic ceiling for any result.

04

Age & likely future loss

A plan has to account for the hair you may still lose — especially at a younger age — so the result ages well.

05

Scalp health & history

Active symptoms, scarring or medical history are assessed before any elective procedure is considered.

06

Realistic goals & maintenance

A limited donor supply means priorities and trade-offs. Honest expectations are part of a good plan.

Diagnosis Before Technique

FUE is a method. It is not the diagnosis.

Technique selection follows assessment, not the other way around. FUE (follicular unit extraction) removes individual follicular units; FUT uses a donor strip. Neither is universally better — the right choice depends on your donor area, goals and case.

Extraction method, safe donor management, graft handling, recipient-site planning and future hair loss all matter more than a brand name.

What Happens During Consultation

Four stages, in order.

01

Understand the loss

History, pattern, progression, scalp and relevant causes.

02

Examine the donor area

Density, miniaturisation, hair characteristics, scarring and safe supply.

03

Plan the long term

Hairline, coverage priorities, future loss, medical maintenance and alternatives.

04

Discuss the procedure

Technique, graft planning after examination, recovery, risks, limitations and cost.

Cost, Honestly

Why an honest cost cannot begin with one photograph.

A meaningful estimate depends on your plan and examination — not a picture. It may depend on:

  • Treatment plan & technique — what is actually recommended for you
  • Safe graft requirement & donor availability — the finite supply you have
  • Hairline versus crown priorities — where limited grafts are best spent
  • Primary versus revision case — revisions are more complex
  • Procedure complexity & time
  • Team, facility, aftercare and follow-up
  • Whether non-surgical treatment is recommended first
